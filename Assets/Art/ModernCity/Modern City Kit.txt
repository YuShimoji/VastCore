Modern City Kit

Open ModernCity.unity, select Play, then click the Game view to walk with WASD and look with the mouse. Esc releases the mouse. Both analog sticks are supported.
Vastcore > Modern City > Variants builds a new street scene or adds a configurable building to the active scene.
Prefabs/Buildings, Prefabs/Street and Prefabs/Props can be placed independently. Wall bays face -Z. The 4 m bay and 3 m storey mate at nominal slab height. Leave the rear 4 m bay open above each stair. The demo uses Unity 6 URP and the Input System.
